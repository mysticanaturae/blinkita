In the metaphysical language of BLINKITA, we understand the body as a collection of information, experiences and memories through which our current identity expresses itself.

The body is not simply something we carry with us. It is the space through which we experience the world, ourselves, relationships, movement, pleasure, boundaries and change.

When our inner relationship with ourselves changes, the way we experience, use and inhabit our body begins to change as well.

That is why BLINKITA BODY is not only about the body. It is about the possibility of creating a new relationship with ourselves through the body — and opening space for a new identity.

BLINKITA BODY · BODY · MEMORY · TRANSFORMATION

Your body is not an obstacle.
It is a space of memory.

The body is the first space in which life takes place. It is where sensation, experience, habits, movement, touch and the way we experience ourselves meet.

BLINKITA BODY explores the body as a living system of information and as a space that can support us in changing our identity — not by fighting against ourselves, but by beginning to reconnect with our body.

BLINKITA · METAPHYSICAL PERSPECTIVE

The renewal of the body as a symbol of transformation.

Some metaphysical traditions use the image of the body gradually renewing itself through approximately seven-year cycles. BLINKITA uses this as a symbolic map of change: what you are living today does not necessarily have to be what you live tomorrow.

In the BLINKITA language, this also includes the symbolic concept of Homo lumionus and its shorter, eight-month metaphysical renewal. This is not a scientific claim about the actual rate at which human cells are replaced, but a consciously used metaphysical language for exploring identity, renewal and inner transformation.
