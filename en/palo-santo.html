01 · WHAT PREMIUM MEANS

Premium begins with origin.

At BLINKITA, we are not talking only about a fragrant stick. We are talking about the wood itself: its origin, maturation, aroma and the way it reached you.

Our premium selection is based on wood from naturally fallen branches. The tree is not cut down to create the product. The branch completes its natural cycle. Drying time, maturation conditions and careful selection of individual pieces are all part of the premium standard.

For selected premium wood, the aim is to collect the pieces without prolonged contact with soil. Wood exposed to moisture and soil for long periods can begin to deteriorate — just as any natural wood does.

02 · MORE THAN INCENSE

When you stop seeing the stick as only incense, another world opens.

Palo Santo has a long history of traditional and folk uses. The ideas below are presented as ritual or traditional practices, not as medical instructions. For internal use, use only a product explicitly labelled as suitable for food use.

01 · SMOKE · SPACE

The familiar use is aromatic fumigation of a space or personal ritual. In BLINKITA ritual language, this is a moment of clearing and preparing space for a new intention.

02 · WATER · AROMA

The wood is sometimes used to scent water as a ritual experience. For drinking, use only material explicitly suitable for food use. Essential oil is not intended for drinking.

03 · TEA · TRADITION

You may encounter traditional preparations described as Palo Santo tea. Treat these as ethnobotanical practices, not medical therapy. Any product intended for consumption must be appropriately labelled for food use.

04 · HOME · POTATOES

Folk practices also mention aromatic wood in the storage of produce. If you explore this, use a separate clean piece intended for the purpose — never a piece that has already been burned or used as incense.

05 · CHARCOAL · ORAL CARE

Small charred particles appear in some traditional oral-care practices. This is not a substitute for brushing or dental care, and we do not claim that it restores enamel. Charcoal can be abrasive.

06 · FEMININE RITUALS

Vaginal steaming with wood or herbs is not a proven medical treatment and can cause burns or irritation. BLINKITA therefore does not present it as a medical procedure and does not recommend directing steam, smoke or essential oils into the vaginal area.

07 · INTENTION · EVERYDAY RITUAL

The simplest use can be the most personal: scent, smoke, a moment of stillness and a clear intention. In BLINKITA language, Palo Santo is not only about clearing what is old, but also about making space for what you consciously want to invite in.

BLINKITA RITUAL LANGUAGE

In BLINKITA, Palo Santo is not presented as a “magic stick”, but as a ritual companion. First you create space. Then you decide what you want to bring into it.

BOTANICAL NOTE

Bursera graveolens is an accepted botanical species in the Burseraceae family. Its native range extends from Mexico to north-western Venezuela and Peru. Kew records social, material, traditional medicinal and food uses for the species.

Because the common name “Palo Santo” is used for several different species, knowing the exact botanical identity matters when selecting a premium product.
