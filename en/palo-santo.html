01 · WHAT PREMIUM MEANS

Premium begins where the story begins.

At BLINKITA, we do not look only at a scented stick. We look at the wood. Its origin. Its maturation. Its dryness, structure, aroma and character. And above all — the journey it has taken from its natural cycle to your space.

Our premium selection is based on wood from naturally fallen branches. The tree is not cut down in order to create a product. The branch is allowed to complete its natural cycle. Drying time, maturation conditions and careful selection of individual pieces are also important to our premium selection.

With selected wood, it is also important how long it has been exposed to moisture and soil. Palo Santo is a natural material, and natural materials have their limits. That is why we look for pieces that are sufficiently dry, stable and suitable for further use — without trying to erase the traces of their origin.

02 · MORE THAN INCENSE

When you stop seeing Palo Santo as simply incense, a different world opens.

Palo Santo has a long history of traditional, folk and ceremonial uses. BLINKITA explores it through several senses: smoke, aroma, wood, water, fire, space and personal intention. Some uses come from traditional practices and are understood here as rituals, not as medical therapies. For consumption, use only material that is explicitly intended for food use.

01 · SMOKE · SPACE

When you light Palo Santo, you are not simply lighting wood. You are creating a moment. Let its characteristic smoke unfold, then extinguish the flame and allow the space to hold the aroma for a few moments. In the BLINKITA language, this is a threshold: first you make space, then you decide what you want to bring into it.

02 · WATER · AROMA

Water is one of the most interesting ways to experience the aroma of wood differently. In some traditional and personal practices, Palo Santo is used to aromatize water. If the intention is consumption, the material must be explicitly suitable and labeled for food use. Essential oil is a concentrated essence and is not intended for drinking.

03 · TEA · TRADITION

Palo Santo also appears in traditional drink preparations. Such practices are an interesting part of the ethnobotanical history of the plant, but we do not present them as treatments. If you prepare a drink, use only a product that is explicitly intended for food use.

04 · HOME · STORAGE

Palo Santo also appears in various household and folk practices. One interesting use is associated with storing produce, where aromatic wood becomes part of an everyday household ritual. If you explore such a practice, use a clean, separate piece of wood intended for that purpose.

05 · CHARCOAL · ORAL CARE

Charred wood has its own distinctive texture and also appears in some traditional oral-care practices. BLINKITA understands this as a folk practice, not as a substitute for modern dental care. Because charcoal can be abrasive, gentleness and moderation are important when exploring such a use.

06 · ŽENSKI RITUALI

Ženski rituali imajo v različnih kulturah dolgo zgodovino. BLINKITA vaginalno parjenje razume kot tradicionalno prakso telesne pozornosti, topline in zavestnega časa zase — ne kot medicinsko zdravljenje. Če se zanj odločiš, naj bo pristop izjemno nežen, brez neposrednega izpostavljanja občutljivih tkiv vročini, dimu ali koncentriranim eteričnim oljem, in nikoli kot nadomestilo za zdravstveno obravnavo.

07 · INTENTION · EVERYDAY LIFE

The simplest use is often the most personal. You light the wood. You open a window. You breathe. For a few moments, you slow the pace of the day. Palo Santo can become a sign that something is ending — or that something new is just beginning.

08 · SCENT · MEMORY

Scent remembers more than we think. A particular aroma can instantly bring back a place, a person, a period or a feeling. Palo Santo has a distinctive aromatic character, and over time it can become part of your personal memory — a scent that marks a moment, a transition or the beginning of a new chapter.

09 · WOOD · FIRE RITUAL

Fire changes the atmosphere in an instant. Palo Santo can accompany a moment when you are completing something, honoring something or simply wanting a different feeling in a space. Use it on a non-combustible surface, never leave it unattended, and make sure it is completely extinguished afterwards.

ORIGIN · JOURNEY · RESPONSIBILITY

Origin · Journey · Responsibility

Our Palo Santo originates in Peru, where it forms part of the natural and cultural heritage of South America. The wood is shipped from Slovenia, where it is carefully prepared and sent to you as part of the BLINKITA ritual experience.

For us, it matters that Palo Santo does not become another story of mass production and the exploitation of natural resources. Its value lies precisely in its connection to living ecosystems, traditional knowledge and the people who know and protect this sacred wood.

With every purchase, you support a more sustainable way of sourcing Palo Santo and the local communities involved in preserving it, rather than a model in which large corporations could exploit the resource through large-scale logging driven purely by short-term profit.

Our intention is simple: for Palo Santo to remain a living heritage, not become a mass-produced commodity.

That is why we give importance to responsible sourcing, traceability and methods of obtaining the wood that respect nature, local knowledge and the future of this sacred tree.
