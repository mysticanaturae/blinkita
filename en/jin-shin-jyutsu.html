01 · WHAT IS JIN SHIN JYUTSU?

Your hands are always with you.

Jin Shin Jyutsu is a Japanese art of harmonising the body's energy through gentle touch, awareness and presence.

In self-help practice, we use our own hands and fingers as a simple way to pause, feel the body and create space for greater ease and balance.

The touch is gentle. Breath, awareness and the ability to observe what is happening in the body are part of the practice.

You do not have to understand the body first. You can begin by listening to it.
